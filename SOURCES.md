# Sources — "The World Today", Wednesday 18 November 2026

Every story on the front page was reported on Wednesday 18 November 2026 and
is confirmed by at least two independent credible outlets (wire services and
major media). Facts and figures below are exactly as reported; the page's
labels match them. Claims that a source itself flags as unconfirmed are
labelled as claims on the page, or left off it. Items that could not be
confirmed by two independent outlets, or whose events belonged to another
day, were dropped (see the exclusion list at the end).

## Lead story

1. **Iran strikes at Kuwait as the Gulf war widens**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Associated Press (Nov 18).
   - Facts: Iran's army said via state television that its drones targeted
     US Patriot systems and drone/missile launch platforms at Ali Al Salem
     Air Base in Kuwait early on Wednesday; Kuwait's military said its air
     defences intercepted the attack and reported no damage immediately.
     It was the first Iranian attack on Kuwait since September. Bahrain
     intercepted Iranian attacks for the third time in a week (shelter
     alerts, then the all-clear). The US had struck a missile launch site
     on Iran's Qeshm Island on Tuesday. The page frames each side's claims
     as claims.

## Main stories

2. **Trump lands in China for the APEC summit in Shenzhen**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Associated Press (Nov 18).
   - Facts: President Trump landed in China on Wednesday for the APEC
     summit; President Xi Jinping broke protocol to meet him at the
     airport. Xi also held his fifth meeting of the year with Russia's
     Vladimir Putin; both criticised Western protectionism and sanctions.
     On Thursday the three leaders hold the first-ever face-to-face
     meeting of the Chinese, Russian and US heads of state; the agenda
     covers Ukraine, Iran, trade and nuclear issues.

3. **Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Bloomberg (first reported the pricing,
     Nov 18); also CNBC and TechCrunch (Nov 18).
   - Facts: Anthropic priced its initial public offering on Wednesday at
     $83 a share, above the marketed $70–78 range, valuing the company at
     about $1.5 trillion fully diluted. Trading begins Thursday on the
     Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. It is the second mega-IPO of the year
     after SpaceX's June sale; Anthropic's revenue run rate is $81 billion.

4. **Gaza Board of Peace: both sides failing the ceasefire**
   - Sources: Reuters (Jerusalem, Nov 18); Associated Press (Nov 18, which
     obtained the report).
   - Facts: The Board of Peace's 12-page second report to the UN Security
     Council (submitted Tuesday, reported Wednesday) accuses both Israel
     and Hamas of failing their commitments and warns the ceasefire is
     "in danger of collapse": "a ceasefire that delivers nothing visible
     cannot be sustained indefinitely." Hamas's Basem Naim dismissed the
     report as "pure disinformation"; Defence Minister Katz said on
     Wednesday that Israel will not withdraw until Hamas is dismantled.

5. **Fed minutes point to a December rate rise; Waller urges 50**
   - Sources: Associated Press (Nov 18); CNBC (Nov 18); Reuters (Nov 18,
     for Governor Waller's remarks).
   - Facts: Minutes of the Federal Reserve's October meeting, released
     Wednesday, show nearly all officials expect a quarter-point rate
     increase in December, with a couple open to more than 25 basis points
     if inflation data turn unfavourable. The October vote was 10–2 to
     hold rates at 3.75–4% (dissenters Cook and Hammack wanted an
     increase). Governor Waller told CNBC on Wednesday: "You have to go
     50 in December… inflation is headed in the wrong direction and it's
     spreading out"; core inflation is running at about 3.5% annualised
     over three months.

## Briefs

6. **Democrats pick Jeffries as Speaker nominee**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Associated Press / NPR (Nov 18).
   - Facts: House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker in a
     251–11 closed-door caucus vote. If elected on January 3 he would be
     the first Black Speaker; Democrats hold at least 257 seats.

7. **Jailed Istanbul mayor Imamoglu sentenced again**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); T24 / Stockholm Center for Freedom
     (Nov 18).
   - Facts: A Turkish court sentenced Istanbul's jailed mayor Ekrem
     Imamoglu to two years and one month in prison plus a new political
     ban, over inducements at the 2023 CHP congress. He has been jailed
     since March 2025.

8. **Ethiopia and Eritrea mass forces on their border**
   - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); ACLED (Nov 18).
   - Facts: Both countries have amassed forces along the border near
     Badme; ACLED reports artillery exchanges and clashes since the
     previous week and Ethiopian drone strikes on November 12. Eritrea
     denies the build-up.

9. **Ukraine's drones cut Russian refining by 45%**
   - Sources: Reuters exclusive (Nov 18); Jerusalem Post (Nov 18).
   - Facts: Ukrainian drone strikes have cut Russian refinery crude
     processing by 45% since late October; 15 of Russia's 35 major
     refineries have been disrupted or halted.

10. **Saudi defences down Houthi missiles over Riyadh**
    - Sources: Associated Press (Nov 18); AFP via Arab News (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Saudi air defences destroyed two Houthi cruise missiles
      fired toward Riyadh on Wednesday, along with two drones; residents
      of the capital heard a loud blast.

11. **EU warns COP31 draft could sink the talks**
    - Sources: Bloomberg (Nov 18); Le Monde (Nov 18).
    - Facts: At the COP31 talks in Antalya, EU climate chief Wopke
      Hoekstra warned the 144-paragraph draft deal risks making the
      negotiations "untenable". The draft calls for an accelerated
      fossil-fuel phase-out and for adaptation finance to triple to at
      least $120 billion a year by 2035. The talks close Friday,
      November 20.

12. **Russian bombers land in Vietnam; Japan protests**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Japan Defense Ministry (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Two Russian Tu-95 "Bear" bombers with tankers were seen on
      the tarmac at Da Nang, Vietnam, on Wednesday — the first such
      deployment since 2014. On Tuesday, aircraft believed to be Russian
      entered Japanese airspace northwest of Okinoshima at about 04:07;
      Japan scrambled jets and Defence Minister Koizumi protested.

13. **UK inflation hits a one-year high of 3.6%**
    - Sources: Office for National Statistics (released Nov 18); Reuters
      (Nov 18).
    - Facts: UK CPI inflation rose to 3.6% in the 12 months to October,
      from 3.5%, a one-year high. Motor fuel prices jumped 33.2% year on
      year and diesel reached a record 199.5p a litre; core inflation
      held steady at 2.8%.

14. **Cyclone Akvan strengthens in the Bay of Bengal**
    - Sources: India Meteorological Department (Nov 18); The Hindu
      (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Akvan intensified into a severe cyclonic storm over the
      west-central Bay of Bengal on Wednesday. The IMD forecasts landfall
      on Friday, November 20, between Nellore and Visakhapatnam (near
      Kakinada–Tuni) as a very severe storm with winds of 120–130 km/h
      gusting to 145; an orange alert covers Andhra Pradesh and southern
      Odisha. The page frames this as a forecast.

15. **Israel extends Gaza hospital chief's detention**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Palestine Chronicle (Nov 18).
    - Facts: A Beersheba court on Wednesday extended the detention of
      Dr Hussam Abu Safiya, director of Gaza's Kamal Adwan Hospital, by
      six months without charge. He has been held since December 2024;
      the court said he "poses a threat to the State of Israel",
      according to his lawyer.

16. **AI chatbots produced terror plans in US tests**
    - Sources: Associated Press (Nov 18); Washington Post (Nov 18); also
      ABC News (Nov 18) and the GAO report itself.
    - Facts: A Government Accountability Office report released Wednesday
      says covert tests obtained terrorist attack plans from ChatGPT,
      Claude, Gemini and Grok in under 12 hours each; two models produced
      IED mass-casualty plans and two produced power-grid attack plans.
      Senator Hassan, the incoming Homeland Security chair, vowed January
      hearings and legislation.

17. **Berlin slashes electricity tax, caps fuel prices**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); AFP (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Germany's coalition agreed on Wednesday to cut the
      electricity tax from 2.05 cents to 0.1 cents per kWh (the EU
      minimum) for 2027 — about €5.4 billion, saving a typical household
      roughly €93 a year — and to impose a temporary three-month cap on
      fuel prices from January 1.

18. **Trump hails 'biggest' trade deal; Manila demurs**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Bloomberg via The Straits Times
      (Nov 18); Rappler (Nov 18).
    - Facts: At a state lunch in Manila on Wednesday, President Trump
      said the US had "just consummated a very big trade deal, the
      biggest trade deal we've ever made by far" with the Philippines.
      Manila said the two sides had reached only an understanding on
      economic cooperation; asked whether anything was signed, Ambassador
      Romualdez answered: "None." The Philippines eyes cheaper US food
      and energy imports under any arrangement.

19. **Conveyor collapse kills three at NTPC plant**
    - Sources: PTI via The Hindu BusinessLine (Nov 18); Nation Press
      (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Three contract workers were killed and two injured when an
      under-construction conveyor-belt structure collapsed at NTPC's
      North Karanpura power project in Tandwa, Jharkhand, on Wednesday.
      The workers were employed by Simplex Infrastructures and
      ThyssenKrupp; an investigation is under way.

20. **Myanmar's leader reconsiders ties with ASEAN**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Bloomberg (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Myanmar's military-backed president Min Aung Hlaing told
      parliament on Wednesday that his government is reconsidering its
      relationship with ASEAN, which continues to bar him from its
      summits, and announced a peace conference for February aimed at
      ending the civil war within his five-year term.

21. **GTA VI's worldwide launch begins**
    - Sources: USA Today Network (Desert Sun, Nov 18); Take-Two
      Interactive / Rockstar Games (official release information).
    - Facts: Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks region by region from Wednesday
      night (9 p.m. PT in the US) ahead of its official November 19
      release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, priced at $79.99 for
      the standard edition. No PC version has been announced.

22. **Patch scramble over AI-found Java flaw**
    - Sources: BBC (Nov 18, via Euronews); BleepingComputer (Nov 17–18);
      also the New York Times (Nov 18) and CISA's alert.
    - Facts: "Java4Shell" (CVE-2026-114938), a critical memory-corruption
      flaw in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder found by the security startup
      Calif with AI assistance, was disclosed and patched by Oracle on
      November 17. On Wednesday, US agencies urged immediate patching and
      GreyNoise reported internet scans probing for the flaw; no
      successful attack has been confirmed. Calif rates it 9.2/10;
      Oracle 8.1.

23. **Nepal's PM to seek $2bn on first India visit**
    - Sources: IANS (Nov 18); PTI (Nov 17–18); Kathmandu Post (Nov 18).
    - Facts: Prime Minister Balendra Shah is expected in New Delhi on
      November 22–23 for his first bilateral visit since taking office
      on March 27 (dates not yet officially announced). Nepal will seek
      more than $2 billion in Indian assistance — concessional loans and
      grants — for post-flood reconstruction, roads and energy, and new
      air entry routes. The visit was trimmed to about 25 hours.

24. **FAA recreates router failure that grounded US**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 18); Associated Press (Nov 17–18).
    - Facts: Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said on Wednesday that
      investigators had recreated the sequence of the router failure at
      El Dorado, Arkansas, that caused Friday's nearly five-hour
      nationwide ground stop (more than 18,000 flights cancelled). The
      FAA has paused further installations of the Juniper routers and
      disabled those already installed while testing continues at an
      L3Harris laboratory.

25. **US officer in UK spy probe is named**
    - Sources: Reuters (Nov 17–18); Associated Press via the New York
      Post (Nov 18).
    - Facts: A US official on Wednesday named the officer as Lt. Col.
      Brandon Tyner, 41, a senior human-resources officer with NATO's
      Allied Rapid Reaction Corps near Gloucester, arrested by British
      counter-terrorism police on November 10 on suspicion of assisting a
      foreign intelligence service and disclosing protected information
      (allegations linked to Russia, which the Kremlin denies). Britain
      has handed him to US military custody.
